About WastageDetection

An AI-powered platform for monitoring and detecting aquatic waste using state-of-the-art object detection models

Our Mission

WastageDetection helps municipalities, environmental organizations, and researchers quickly detect and quantify aquatic waste using advanced AI. Our goal is to accelerate cleanup efforts, prioritize resources, and measure environmental impact with scientific precision.

By leveraging computer vision and machine learning, we transform how organizations monitor and protect aquatic ecosystems from water hyacinths, floating waste, and other environmental threats.

Key Features

Real-time Live Detection

Stream directly from cameras or upload video files for instant analysis and overlay detection results.

Historical Analytics

Track detection patterns over time with comprehensive analytics, trends, and comparative insights.

Interactive Maps & Reports

Visualize detection hotspots on maps and generate detailed reports for cleanup planning.

Model Selection

Choose from multiple AI models and tune confidence thresholds for your specific use case.

High-Performance Processing

Process images at 45+ FPS with low-latency inference for real-time environmental monitoring.

Enterprise Security

Secure data handling, encrypted communications, and compliance with environmental data standards.
