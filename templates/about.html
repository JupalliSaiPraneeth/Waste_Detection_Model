About WaterGuard AI

An AI platform for monitoring and detecting floating waste in waterways using state-of-the-art object detection models.

Mission

WaterGuard AI helps municipalities, environmental organizations and researchers quickly detect and quantify floating waste in rivers, lakes and canals so cleanup efforts can be prioritized and measured.

Key Features

  • Real-time live detection
  • Historical detection analytics
  • Interactive maps & reports
  • Model selection and confidence tuning
